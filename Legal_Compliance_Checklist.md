# Legal Compliance Checklist for Temporary Employment Agency
## Netherlands Regulatory Requirements

### 1. Business Registration & Licensing

#### Chamber of Commerce (KvK) Registration
- [ ] Register company with KvK
- [ ] Obtain KvK number
- [ ] Register trade name
- [ ] Submit business plan
- [ ] Pay registration fees

#### Dutch Labor Authority (NLA) License
- [ ] Apply for NLA license (Wet allocatie arbeidskrachten)
- [ ] Submit required documentation:
  - [ ] Business plan
  - [ ] Financial statements
  - [ ] Management CVs
  - [ ] Compliance procedures
- [ ] Pay license fees (€2,500 - €5,000)
- [ ] Await approval (4-8 weeks)

#### Tax Registration
- [ ] Register with Dutch Tax Authority (Belastingdienst)
- [ ] Obtain VAT number (BTW)
- [ ] Register for payroll tax (Loonheffingen)
- [ ] Set up digital tax filing
