## Legal Compliance Framework

### Dutch Labor Law Requirements
1. **NLA License:** Mandatory registration with Dutch Labor Authority
2. **Collective Labor Agreements:** Compliance with sector-specific CAOs
3. **Working Conditions:** Adherence to Working Conditions Act (Arbowet)
4. **Equal Treatment:** Compliance with Equal Treatment Act
5. **Data Protection:** GDPR compliance for employee data

### Required Registrations
- **KvK Registration:** Chamber of Commerce
- **Tax Registration:** Dutch Tax Authority (Belastingdienst)
- **NLA License:** Dutch Labor Authority
- **Sector-Specific Permits:** Healthcare, construction, etc.
