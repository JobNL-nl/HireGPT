## REVENUE MODEL AND ASSUMPTIONS {#revenue-model}

### Revenue Stream Analysis

#### Primary Revenue Streams

**1. Temporary Placement Fees (70% of total revenue)**
- **Model:** Markup on hourly wages paid to temporary workers
- **Typical Markup:** 15-35% depending on skill level and industry
- **Average Markup Used:** 25%
- **Revenue Recognition:** Monthly based on hours worked

**2. Permanent Placement Fees (18% of total revenue)**
- **Model:** Percentage of first-year salary for permanent hires
- **Fee Range:** 15-25% of annual salary
- **Average Fee Used:** 20%
- **Revenue Recognition:** Upon successful placement and probation completion

**3. Payroll Services (8% of total revenue)**
- **Model:** Administrative fee for payroll processing
- **Fee Range:** 2-4% of gross payroll
- **Average Fee Used:** 3%
- **Revenue Recognition:** Monthly processing fees

**4. Additional Services (4% of total revenue)**
- **Training and Development:** €150-€500 per participant
- **Recruitment Consulting:** €2,000-€10,000 per project
- **HR Advisory Services:** €150-€300 per hour
- **Technology Platform Licensing:** €500-€2,000 per month

### Key Business Assumptions

#### Market and Growth Assumptions
- **Dutch Temporary Employment Market Growth:** 4% annually
- **Market Share Capture:** 0.05% by Year 3, 0.08% by Year 5
- **Client Retention Rate:** 85% annually
- **Average Client Relationship Duration:** 3.5 years
- **New Client Acquisition:** 25% growth annually (Years 1-3)

#### Operational Assumptions
- **Average Assignment Duration:** 4 months
- **Worker Utilization Rate:** 75% (percentage of time workers are placed)
- **Client Payment Terms:** Net 30 days
- **Bad Debt Rate:** 1.5% of revenue
- **Seasonal Variation:** 15% higher Q4, 10% lower Q1

#### Cost Assumptions
- **Staff Cost Inflation:** 3% annually
- **Office Rent Increases:** 2% annually
- **Technology Costs:** 5% annual increase for enhancements
- **Marketing ROI:** €4 revenue for every €1 marketing spend

### Revenue Calculation Methodology

#### Temporary Placement Revenue Formula
```
Monthly Revenue = (Number of Active Workers × Average Hours per Month × Average Hourly Rate × Markup Percentage)
```

**Example Calculation (Month 12):**
- Active Workers: 180
- Average Hours/Month: 160
- Average Hourly Rate: €18
- Markup: 25%
- Monthly Revenue: 180 × 160 × €18 × 0.25 = €129,600

#### Permanent Placement Revenue Formula
```
Annual Revenue = (Number of Permanent Placements × Average Annual Salary × Fee Percentage)
```

**Example Calculation (Year 1):**
- Permanent Placements: 24
- Average Salary: €45,000
- Fee Percentage: 20%
- Annual Revenue: 24 × €45,000 × 0.20 = €216,000
