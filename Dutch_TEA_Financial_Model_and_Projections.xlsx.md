## Risk Assessment and Mitigation

### Financial Risks

#### Market Risk
- **Risk:** Economic downturn reducing staffing demand
- **Impact:** 20-30% revenue reduction
- **Mitigation:** Client diversification, cost control measures

#### Credit Risk
- **Risk:** Client payment delays or defaults
- **Impact:** Cash flow disruption
- **Mitigation:** Credit checks, payment terms, insurance

#### Operational Risk
- **Risk:** Inability to find qualified workers
- **Impact:** Lost revenue opportunities
- **Mitigation:** Worker database development, training programs

### Contingency Planning

#### Revenue Shortfall Scenarios
- **10% Revenue Reduction:** Reduce marketing spend, delay hiring
- **20% Revenue Reduction:** Implement cost-cutting measures
- **30% Revenue Reduction:** Seek additional funding, restructure operations

#### Cost Overrun Scenarios
- **10% Cost Increase:** Optimize operations, renegotiate contracts
- **20% Cost Increase:** Reduce discretionary spending
- **30% Cost Increase:** Seek additional funding sources
