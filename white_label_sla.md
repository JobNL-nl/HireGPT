## 1. Purpose
The Principal is an IND-recognised sponsor authorised to employ non-EU workers in the Netherlands. The Subcontractor specialises in sourcing, recruiting and arranging visa documentation for such workers.  Under this Agreement the Subcontractor will supply workers and related services to the Principal, who will act as the formal employer and sponsor.

## 2. Scope of Services
2.1 Sourcing & Screening of Candidates  
2.2 Collection, translation & legalisation of documentation  
2.3 Preparation and submission of TWV/MVV/GVVA applications on behalf of Principal  
2.4 Travel & arrival coordination (airport pick-up, BSN, housing)  
2.5 First-week onboarding & integration support  
2.6 Optional payroll & HR administration as agreed in Schedule 1

## 3. Responsibilities of the Principal
3.1 Maintain IND sponsor status, pay annual supervision fees and comply with all sponsor obligations under the Aliens Decree 2000.  
3.2 Sign the employment contract with each Worker and ensure timely salary payment meeting the applicable salary threshold and CAO.  
3.3 Provide the Subcontractor with Power of Attorney (Form 1078) to file applications.  
3.4 Notify the IND of any changes in employment within 4 weeks.  
3.5 Allow the Subcontractor reasonable access to HR records for compliance audits.

## 4. Responsibilities of the Subcontractor
4.1 Deliver complete and truthful application files, free of forged or misleading documents.  
4.2 Observe GDPR when processing personal data of Workers.  
4.3 Maintain digital records of passports, pay slips, housing contracts, and make them available to the Principal or authorities upon request within 24 hours.  
4.4 Provide replacement candidates or refund the Service Fee if an application is refused due to Subcontractor error.

## 5. Fees & Payment
5.1 **Service Fee:** €________ per Worker, payable 14 days after IND approval.  
5.2 **Success Bonus (optional):** €________ payable 30 days after Worker has completed 3 months’ employment.  
5.3 All invoices are exclusive of VAT. Late payments incur 1 % interest per month.

## 6. Compliance & Audits
6.1 Either Party may conduct (or appoint a third-party to conduct) compliance audits once per calendar year upon 10 business-day notice.  
6.2 Non-conformities must be remedied within 30 days.  Failure may lead to suspension of new applications.

## 7. Liability & Indemnity
7.1 Each Party indemnifies the other against fines or claims arising from its own breach of immigration or employment law.  
7.2 Liability is capped at the total Fees paid in the last 12 months, except for wilful misconduct or fraud.

## 8. Confidentiality
Both Parties shall keep all non-public information confidential for 5 years after termination.

## 9. Term & Termination
9.1 Initial term: 12 months from Effective Date.  
9.2 Either Party may terminate with 60 days’ written notice.  
9.3 Immediate termination for serious breach, loss of sponsor status, insolvency or illegal conduct.

## 10. Non-Solicitation
Neither Party shall directly employ or contract personnel of the other Party involved in this Agreement during the term and 12 months thereafter, without prior written consent.

## 11. Governing Law & Disputes
Dutch law applies.  Disputes are submitted to the competent court of Amsterdam.

## 12. Miscellaneous
12.1 Amendments must be in writing and signed by both Parties.  
12.2 If any provision is held invalid, the remaining provisions remain in effect.  
12.3 Schedules form an integral part of this Agreement.
